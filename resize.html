Why Image Dimensions Matter for Social Media

Every social media platform uses its own proprietary compression algorithm. If you upload a photo that doesn't match their required aspect ratio, the platform will crop it automatically—often cutting off heads or important text. Our tool ensures your photos are pixel-perfect before you hit upload.

?? Instagram Guides

Use 1080x1080 for square posts, but 1080x1350 for portraits to take up the maximum screen real estate in the feed.

?? TikTok & Reels

Always use 1080x1920 (9:16). Ensure your "safe zones" are respected so UI elements don't cover your photo.

?? YouTube Thumbnails

Stick to 1280x720. Keep your file size under 2MB to ensure compatibility with YouTube's upload limits.
