Why Use a Bulk Image Converter?

Converting images individually is time-consuming. PhotoSplit Studio's free Bulk Image Converter allows you to drop dozens of JPEG, PNG, or WebP images and convert them all at once. Everything happens locally in your browser, meaning your files are never uploaded to a server, ensuring 100% privacy and blazing fast speeds. Whether you are optimizing images for a website by converting PNG to WebP, or standardizing client photos to JPEG, our tool handles it securely.

Supported Formats

  • JPEG: Best for photographs. Offers great compression with minimal quality loss.
  • PNG: Best for graphics with transparency or crisp edges. Lossless but larger file size.
  • WebP: The modern web standard. Offers superior compression compared to JPEG and supports transparency like PNG.
