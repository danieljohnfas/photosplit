About PhotoSplit Studio

A free, privacy-first browser tool for digitizing your family memories instantly.

The Privacy-First Mission

Most online photo tools require you to upload your precious family memories to their servers. We think that's a security risk you shouldn't have to take.

By utilizing WebAssembly and OpenCV.js, we brought professional-grade computer vision directly into your browser. Your photos never leave your RAM, and they certainly never touch our servers.

Why We Built PhotoSplit Studio

"Every day, thousands of physical photos are lost to time, fire, or fading. Scanning is the first step, but splitting those scans into individual files is usually a tedious chore that requires expensive software like Photoshop. We built PhotoSplit Studio to democratize archiving. We wanted to remove that friction so anyone, regardless of technical skill, can successfully preserve their family history for the next generation."

— The PhotoSplit Development Team

Meet the Team

PhotoSplit Studio is maintained by a dedicated group of software engineers, digital archivists, and photography enthusiasts. We noticed a massive gap in the market: existing photo splitting tools either required desktop installations, cost money, or aggressively tracked user data. We set out to build a suite of tools that we would actually want to use ourselves—fast, free, and obsessively focused on privacy.

📸 Digital Archiving Experts

Our team consults with professional archivists to ensure our algorithms meet strict preservation standards. Whether it's choosing the right JPEG compression ratios or handling massive 600 DPI lossless PNGs, our tools are built to respect the integrity of your original scans.

🔒 Privacy Advocates

We believe your family photos belong to you. That's why we architected PhotoSplit Studio as a client-side application. We do not maintain user databases, we do not require account creation, and we physically cannot view the photos you process through our app.

⚡ Browser Performance

Optimized for modern multi-core processors using WebAssembly. Split 10+ photos from a single scan in less than 2 seconds without ever leaving your browser tab.

🔒 Absolute Security

Zero tracking. Zero uploads. Zero storage. Your privacy is baked directly into our architecture. Unplug your router while using the app—it still works.

🎨 Archival Quality

We support lossless PNG exports and provide high-fidelity JPEG compression options to guarantee you maintain archival quality for future generations.
