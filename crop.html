Free Online Image Cropper

Cropping an image is the easiest way to improve composition or fit a photo into a specific frame. PhotoSplit Studio's free online image cropper lets you quickly trim unwanted edges, focus on your subject, and change the aspect ratio of any photo without installing software. Since all processing happens in your browser, your photos remain 100% private and are never uploaded to our servers.

Common Aspect Ratios

  • 1:1 Square: The classic format for Instagram posts and profile pictures.
  • 16:9: The standard widescreen ratio for YouTube thumbnails, presentations, and desktop wallpapers.
  • 4:3: The default aspect ratio for most smartphone cameras and digital point-and-shoots.
  • 9:16: Vertical format used for Instagram Stories, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
