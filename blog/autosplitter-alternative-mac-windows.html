If you're digitizing family archives, you've likely come across AutoSplitter—a popular desktop software designed to split scanned images containing multiple photos. While it gets the job done, it comes with a steep price tag and requires installation on your machine.

Advertisement

But what if you could get the same functionality for free, straight from your browser, without sacrificing your privacy? Enter PhotoSplit Studio.

Why Choose PhotoSplit Studio Over AutoSplitter?

Here are the core reasons why thousands of archivists are making the switch:

  • 100% Free: No paywalls, no trial limits, no watermarks. We believe preserving your family history shouldn't cost a fortune.
  • No Installation Needed: Works directly in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. No `.exe` or `.dmg` files to download.
  • Mac & Windows Compatible: Because it's web-based, it works flawlessly on any operating system, including Linux.
  • Uncompromising Privacy (100% Offline): PhotoSplit Studio uses WebAssembly (WASM) to process images locally on your device. Your precious family photos are never uploaded to a server.

How it Works

The workflow is incredibly simple:

  1. Place 3-4 photos on your flatbed scanner, leaving a small gap between them.
  2. Scan at 300 or 600 DPI and save as a single JPEG or PNG file.
  3. Drag and drop the scanned image into PhotoSplit Studio.
  4. Our OpenCV.js engine automatically detects the edges and splits them into individual high-resolution files.
  5. Click "Download All" to save your separated memories instantly.

Conclusion

If you want a powerful, easy-to-use, and entirely free AutoSplitter alternative, PhotoSplit Studio is built specifically for you. Try it today and speed up your archiving workflow instantly!
